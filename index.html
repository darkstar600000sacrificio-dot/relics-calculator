Perfection formulas
Level 1 status = Current status − (Relic level − 1)
Status perfection (%) = Level 1 status ÷ Level 1 maximum × 100
Overall perfection = Average of the 2 or 3 entered status perfection values (DMG + at least 1 other status)
Mode perfection = Average of Base stat perfection + selected mode stat perfection
Real DMG multiplier = (1 + DMG ÷ 100) × (1 + mode DMG ÷ 100)
Final HP = Base HP × (1 + HP ÷ 100) × (1 + Endless HP ÷ 100)
Level 1 maximums: DMG 10, Raid 10, Challenge Damage 35, Endless Damage 35; HP 10, Endless HP 15, Endless DEF 10. Challenge DEF has a listed maximum of 5 but is not a working HP relic status.
